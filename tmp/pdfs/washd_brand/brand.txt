01 — THE BRAND


Who Washd Is

Washd is a scheduled wash, iron and fold service built around a simple idea we call the
milkman model: we come to a residence on fixed days, collect a resident's sealed bag from
one discreet point in the building, and return it washed, pressed and neatly folded — without
anyone ever having to think about laundry again.

We give people back the one thing laundry quietly steals:
their time.

Our Mission
To remove the weekly burden of laundry from people's lives through a service so reliable and discreet that it
simply runs in the background — building by building, on a schedule residents can depend on.


What Makes Us Different
  • The milkman model. Fixed schedule, one collection point per building — not chaotic on-demand pickups
    at all hours.

  • Off-site, invisible operation. No outlet, no machinery, no space taken over. Just one small, immaculate
    collection point.

  • Zero burden on the building. Our own staff handle every collection and return; full liability sits with us.

  • A service, not a shop. Flexible plans matched to each household, with simple sign-up and updates by
    WhatsApp.


    Positioning statement
    For residents of managed condominiums who have better things to do with their weekends, Washd is a
    scheduled laundry service that collects, cleans and returns laundry to their building on a reliable fixed
    rhythm — delivering the ease of a premium service without the building ever having to lift a finger.

     The 60 / 30 / 10 rule
     Roughly 60% deep teal & white (the calm base), 30% supporting tones (mist panels, slate text), and
     10% champagne gold. Gold is a jewel, not a wall — used for the full stop, key highlights and single
     accents. Overusing gold cheapens it.

Typography

Washd uses a single, clean sans-serif family across everything. It keeps the brand modern, legible and
uncluttered — the visual equivalent of "simple and handled."

  LEVEL                         STYLE                               USE


  Wordmark                      Lowercase, Bold, gold dot           Logo only

  Headline                      Bold, 20–28pt                       Hero lines, poster headlines

  Subhead                       Bold / Medium, 12–15pt              Section titles

  Body                          Regular, 10–11pt, 1.5 line          Paragraph copy

    Digital / web fallback
    Where Helvetica Neue is unavailable (web, apps, non-designer documents), use Arial, Inter or system
    sans-serif. Never mix in decorative or serif fonts — consistency is what makes the brand feel
    established.



Non-English Materials
For Chinese, Japanese and Korean materials, use Noto Sans CJK (SC / JP / KR) to match the clean sans-
serif character of the brand, keeping the washd. wordmark, colours and layout identical across every language.
06 — VOICE


How Washd Speaks

Washd sounds like a calm, capable friend who has taken something off your plate. Warm but
never gushing; confident but never boastful; clear above all.

Our Voice Is
  • Warm & human — we talk about people's weekends and their time, not "laundry solutions."

  • Calm & reassuring — we remove worry; the tone should feel like a weight lifting.

  • Quietly premium — refined, never flashy. Discretion is part of the product.

  • Simple & direct — short sentences, plain words, no jargon.

07 — IN PRACTICE


Applying the Brand

Every piece should feel:
 • Uncluttered — generous space, one clear message, never crammed.

 • Consistent — same logo, colours, type and tone across every format and language.

 • Considered — the calm, premium feel is the product. Cheap-looking design breaks the promise.


Standard building blocks
 • Deep teal header band with the wordmark and a gold underline

 • 3-step "how it works" (Drop → We collect → Collect)

 • "From RM__" price anchor plus the customisation line — never a cluttered price table on print

 • Sign-up block — QR + WhatsApp + email, always together

 • Launch / location line in the footer
